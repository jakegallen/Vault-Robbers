# Vault Robbers, the 10 one-of-one briefs

Fully bespoke, can be off style. Inspired-by archetypes only, our own names and art, never an exact likeness or trademarked character. Each should feel like a show-stopper that breaks the grid.

## 1. The Prince of Thieves (Robin Hood energy)
- Palette: forest greens, leather brown, a flash of gold
- Elements: feathered cap, hood, longbow and quiver, one stolen gold coin
- Vibe: heroic rogue, mid draw or tipping the cap
- Lore: Steals from the vault, gives to the mob.

## 2. The Gentleman (Lupin style master burglar)
- Palette: black and white with a deep red accent
- Elements: top hat, monocle, white gloves, cane, a calling card marked VR
- Vibe: smug, taking a bow
- Lore: He left a thank you note where the diamonds used to be.

## 3. The Vanisher (Cooper style skyjacker)
- Palette: muted tan trench, storm grey, black shades
- Elements: trench coat, dark sunglasses, a briefcase stuffed with cash, a parachute strap
- Vibe: calm, already half gone, mid stride
- Lore: Jumped with the bag and was never seen again.

## 4. The Outlaw (Wild West bandit)
- Palette: dusty browns, red bandana
- Elements: bandana over the face, cowboy hat, two revolvers, a wanted poster behind him
- Vibe: standoff stance
- Lore: Dead or alive, the reward keeps going up.

## 5. The Highwayman
- Palette: midnight blue, black, silver
- Elements: tricorn hat, cape, flintlock pistol, a horse silhouette
- Vibe: pistol raised, stand and deliver
- Lore: Your coin or your evening, traveler.

## 6. The Pirate King (Blackbeard type)
- Palette: deep sea blue, black, gold
- Elements: braided beard with smoking fuses, tricorn with a skull, cutlass, treasure chest
- Vibe: roaring, cutlass up
- Lore: The only vault he trusts is buried.

## 7. The Cat (silent jewel thief)
- Palette: all black with one green laser accent
- Elements: sleek catsuit and mask, grappling line, a laser grid, one huge diamond
- Vibe: suspended mid heist, weaving the lasers
- Lore: Nine lives, one diamond at a time.

## 8. Public Enemy (1930s gangster)
- Palette: charcoal pinstripe, cream, blood red
- Elements: pinstripe suit, fedora, a violin case, a cigar
- Vibe: leaning cool, half smile
- Lore: The feds have a file, and it is very thick.

## 9. The Forty-First (Ali Baba cave thief)
- Palette: desert sand, royal purple, gold
- Elements: turban, oil lamp, curved dagger, a mountain of gold coins, a cracked open cave
- Vibe: wide eyed greed in the treasure cave
- Lore: Forty got in, he is the one who got out.

## 10. The Grave Robber (our macabre house twist)
- Palette: graveyard greys, sickly green, lantern amber
- Elements: our skull face, a shovel, a lantern, a dug up vault, bones
- Vibe: hunched, mid dig, grinning
- Lore: Even the dead keep something worth taking.
