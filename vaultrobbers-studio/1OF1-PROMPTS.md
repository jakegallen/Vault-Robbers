# Vault Robbers, the 10 one-of-one generation prompts

Ready to paste into an image model (Midjourney, DALL-E, Gemini image, or Stable Diffusion). They can be off style, but a shared spine keeps the ten reading as one legendary tier.

## Shared style spine (prepend or append to every prompt)
single full-body character, bold black ink outlines, flat muted hand-drawn cartoon shading, centered, plain dark vault backdrop with a faint gold rim light, moody, 1:1 square, high detail, no text, no watermark

## The ten

1. The Prince of Thieves: a hooded outlaw in forest green with a longbow and quiver, one gold coin in hand, sly grin.
2. The Gentleman: a dapper master burglar in a black tailcoat, top hat, monocle, white gloves, holding a calling card.
3. The Vanisher: a calm figure in a tan trench coat and dark sunglasses, a cash-stuffed briefcase, a parachute strap over the shoulder, mid stride.
4. The Outlaw: a Wild West bandit with a red bandana over the face, cowboy hat, two revolvers drawn, a wanted poster behind.
5. The Highwayman: a caped rider in a tricorn hat raising a flintlock pistol, a horse silhouette in the fog.
6. The Pirate King: a sea robber with a braided black beard and smoking fuses, tricorn with a skull, cutlass raised, a treasure chest.
7. The Cat: a sleek jewel thief in a black catsuit and mask suspended among red laser beams, one huge diamond in hand.
8. Public Enemy: a 1930s gangster in a charcoal pinstripe suit and fedora, a violin case, a cigar, half smile.
9. The Forty-First: a cave thief in a turban holding an oil lamp and a curved dagger, standing before a mountain of gold.
10. The Grave Robber: a hunched skull-faced robber with a shovel and a lantern, digging up a vault, bones around, grinning.

## Finishing
Generate, pick the best, upscale to 2048x2048 PNG with a transparent or dark background, then upload into the matching slot in the Studio Mythic tab. The render pipeline reserves art indices 990 to 999 for these ten.
