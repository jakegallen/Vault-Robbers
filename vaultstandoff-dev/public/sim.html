Vault Standoff, EV / Odds Simulator

Admin tool. Monte-Carlo of the real crowd-bust mechanic (most-crowded door busts each chamber; last party in the vault wins; SPLIT shared per person). Nothing here ships to players.

Real-money rounds seat ZERO house bots: the field is other humans/wallets only, so there is no bot statistical edge to model (bots exist solely for fake-chip preview liveliness). The lever worth modelling is Sybil: one actor spreading tickets across many wallets. A wallet is one finale party, so splitting your tickets across W wallets gives you up to W finale parties instead of 1 (hedging the pot), but each ticket still funds the pot and pays rake.

Set parameters and hit Run.

Model note: each alive ticket picks the least-crowded door with "dodge skill" probability, else random; the most-crowded door busts. The finale is modeled as an equal per-person split among finalists (matches the anti-hedge rule). Win-prob is your chance to hold a finalist; EV uses your expected per-person share of the pot minus your ticket cost.
