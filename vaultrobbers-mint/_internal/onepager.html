VAULT ROBBERS

One-pager · the mint + the daily on-chain skill game · v1

The pitch

A daily, on-chain, skill game where masked robbers out-read a crowd to crack the vault. No house to beat and no bot can farm it, because winning depends on out-thinking other humans. Every play buys and burns ROBINVAULT; a fixed set of 1,000 Vault Robber NFTs give an edge. Built on Robinhood Chain.

The assets

ROBINVAULT (RVLT)
the fuel
ERC-20, ~46% already burned. Bought to play, burned constantly. Supply only falls.
Vault Robbers
the membership
ERC-721, hard cap 1,000. Grants a 25% discount + priority entry.

The mint

  • Price = 2× the contract's RVLT mint cost (a 100% premium), payable in any coin.
  • Base half buys ROBINVAULT and mints the Robber, the collector gets the NFT.
  • Premium half converts to ETH → treasury, which funds game bonuses / guaranteed pots.
  • ~136 of 1,000 minted. Nothing new after 1,000.

The game — The Vault Standoff

  • Daily & async, the 24h day is the board; nobody has to be online at once. One champion per day. Field capped ~100.
  • Descent: each chamber, everyone secretly picks a route; the most-crowded route busts. A live crowd heatmap + a blind window + a bank-or-push choice.
  • Last Door: the final few play a hold-vs-cash nerve showdown to crown one champion, winner-take-all.
  • Agent-proof by design: the outcome depends on the human crowd (bots that pile in self-bust), plus bluffing and daily novelty.

The economy

  • $10 buy-in (any coin) → buys ROBINVAULT = the pot (winner-take-all + small finalist split), paid in ROBINVAULT.
  • Team takes a 2% rake.
  • 100% of creator fees AND 100% of NFT royalties → buy & burn ROBINVAULT.
  • Deflation from fees + royalties + mint; entries recycle to the winner.
Why hold ROBINVAULT: the game is a daily buy engine, every entry buys it, winners are paid in it, and those buys feed the burn. Demand from play, supply only shrinking.

Integrity

Open entry is hostile to attackers: every entry costs money, buys/burns the token, and self-exposes to its own bust. On top: superlinear per-wallet pricing, hidden + blind + anonymized picks, proof-of-personhood on payout, and collusion detection.

Build & roadmap

Contracts (Robinhood 4663): EntryRouter (buys RVLT into the pot + 2% rake + NFT discount/priority), DailyVault (escrow + pull payout), FeeRouter (100% fees + royalties burn), plus the existing NFT + mint hub. Server-authoritative round engine with published-seed, provably-fair settlement.
